## Delivered

A ticket that hits the review cap can now be parked as a draft PR carrying its open findings.

- New `orch park` command, a `parked` phase/status, and a `parked_pr` column.
- `retire` accepts parked tickets.
- Resume of a parked ticket restores its prior phase and records an `unpark` event.
- `orch add` re-opens a retired parked ticket and records a `readd` event. It refuses while a kept checkout or folder trust survives.
- `wait` wakes on park.
- Docs: "Parking a capped ticket" in `skills/orchestration/references/ticket-pipeline.md`, and the `orch park`, `orch add` and `orch show` rows in `skills/orchestration/references/orch-cli.md`.

Changed files: `scripts/orch`, the two reference docs, and new `tests/test_orch_park.py`.
